# 模板：学术套磁邮件

## 变量
- {{recipient}}: 收件人姓名/称谓
- {{my_name}}: 你的姓名
- {{my_institution}}: 你的学校/机构
- {{position}}: 你的身份（PhD student / Researcher）
- {{topic}}: 对方研究领域
- {{specific_work}}: 对方具体论文/项目（体现你读过）
- {{purpose}}: 你的目的（合作 / 加入实验室 / 请教）
- {{background}}: 你的相关背景/技能

## 正文
Subject: Inquiry about {{topic}} – {{my_name}} from {{my_institution}}

Dear {{recipient}},

I hope this email finds you well. My name is {{my_name}}, and I am a {{position}} at {{my_institution}}.

I have been closely following your research on {{topic}}, particularly your recent work on {{specific_work}}. I am impressed by your insights, which align closely with my current research on {{background}}.

I am writing to {{purpose}}. Would you be available for a brief discussion at your convenience?

Thank you for your time. I look forward to your reply.

Best regards,
{{my_name}}
