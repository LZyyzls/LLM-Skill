# 模板：学术跟进邮件

## 变量
- {{recipient}}: 收件人
- {{topic}}: 跟进事项（投稿 / 面试 / 申请）
- {{action}}: 你之前完成的动作（submitted / interviewed）
- {{date}}: 之前动作的日期
- {{my_name}}: 你的姓名
- {{polite_request}}: 具体请求（询问进度 / 补充材料）

## 正文
Subject: Follow-up on {{topic}}

Dear {{recipient}},

I hope you are doing well. I am writing to follow up on my {{topic}}, which I {{action}} on {{date}}.

I fully understand that these processes take time. {{polite_request}}

Thank you for your assistance. Please let me know if you need any further information.

Best regards,
{{my_name}}
